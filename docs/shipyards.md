# Shipbuilding capability audit

The 10 October 2026 audit informs new ship availability in
[the port roster](ports.md#new-ship-availability). The roster generator is the
source of runtime availability; this document records its evidence and limits.
The approved policy enables confirmed classes and the three named regional
abstractions. Provisional offerings remain disabled.

The five game hulls represent vessel roles rather than exact real-world tonnage,
crew or speed. General cargo and feeder construction support the small and
balanced freighter roles. Bulk carriers require bulk construction evidence;
tankers require oil/product-compatible construction, not just LNG bunkering.
Purpose-designed refrigerated cargo ships support the refrigerated role;
ordinary container ships with some reefer plugs do not. A repair dock's maximum
vessel size is not a newbuilding specification. Company headquarters and vessel
ownership do not identify the construction site.

occurs immediately at the selected game port. Guangzhou alone offers all five
classes and is the default selection for players without ships.

## Other ports, no qualifying offering

An empty catalogue means the audit did not establish a matching new cargo ship
offering. It does not mean that a port has no shipyard or that a custom vessel
could never be built there. Historical merchant construction alone does not
establish a present offering.

|---|---|

The policy applies only to new ship purchases. It preserves existing hulls,
operations, auctions and shipyard buybacks, including ships already located at a
port with an empty newbuild catalogue. Used ships and brokered delivery remain
separate future design possibilities.
