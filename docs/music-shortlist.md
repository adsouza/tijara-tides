## Northern Frangistan

Northern Frangistan covers Rotterdam, Antwerp and Hamburg in the game. The
player selected Lord of the Land for its initial port slot after approving its
repeat and Pathfinder handoff. Townsong and Home Was You remain accepted
alternatives.

[Lord of the Land][lord] by Kevin MacLeod is the selected initial port piece,
originally suggested by the player. MacLeod describes lute, flute and percussion,
lasting 3:06 at 112 BPM. Its acoustic character suggests a European trading-town
atmosphere. It offers less orchestral grandeur than the voyage brief, and the
composer's mysterious and suspenseful mood tags merit attention when listening
for the warmer port mood. This is a broad stylistic association rather than a
claim of historical or local authenticity.

[Townsong][townsong] by Alexander Nakarada remains a player-accepted port
alternative. Its happy, positive folk/rock tags, 3:05 duration and 126 BPM suggest
a livelier trading-town atmosphere. These are broad stylistic associations;
neither port piece establishes a specifically Dutch, Belgian or German tradition.

For voyages, the player selected [Pathfinder][pathfinder] by Scott Buckley over
Adventure. Its bold brass, soaring strings, quieter middle and triumphant return
offer a fuller orchestral arrangement, with battle intensity to assess during
final recording checks. Its regional assignment is a player preference, not a
composer claim of local tradition. [Adventure][nakarada-adventure] by Alexander
Nakarada remains an alternative, with Celtic and medieval tags and an exciting,
dramatic mood, 4:37 at 90 BPM. Both official voyage pages were checked on
10 October 2026 and offer free use under CC BY 4.0. Neither recording has been
auditioned here. Full vocal absence, repeated play, loops and fades remain
recording checks.

[Home Was You][home] by Scott Buckley is an alternative port candidate. Piano,
strings and accordion provide a peaceful folk character; later folk percussion
gives way to a quiet piano ending. Its regional assignment is a proposed fit,
not a geographic claim made by the composer.
